# TX-11: The Pflugerville Stop — What August Pfluger's Newest Constituents Got, Versus His Donors

*A short companion piece to ["Campaign Spending as a Portrait of the Donor Class"](2026-luxury-spending.md). It follows one thread: what the filing data says about how August Pfluger treats Pflugerville, one of the towns redistricting added to TX-11.*

## Why Pflugerville, specifically

TX-11 has long been anchored in Midland, Odessa and San Angelo. The redrawn lines for the 2026 cycle add a strip of North Austin, most of Pflugerville, and parts of Round Rock, Cedar Park and Leander. These are suburbs Pfluger never had to campaign in before. He didn't choose to represent Pflugerville; redistricting handed it to him.

That makes campaign spending there a useful test. How much attention does a member of Congress pay to constituents he inherited, compared with the donor network he built over a career in the Permian Basin?

So: does Pfluger's campaign paper trail show him showing up?

## The paper trail

A search of every FEC disbursement record collected for Pfluger's committees for the word "Pflugerville" turns up exactly one campaign stop. Every cycle is included, as are the raw filings through Sept. 30, 2026. The stop:

- **Date:** 2026-01-31, during the Republican primary window.
- **Charge:** a **$756.42** meal charge at **Springhill Restaurant**, 2505 W Pecan St, Pflugerville, expensed by the principal campaign committee.
- **How it's filed:** as "MEAL EXPENSE" under the FEC's "Travel Expenses" category, tagged to the 2026 primary. It's an itemized sub-item of a corporate card payment.
- **Source:** `tx-11/august-pfluger/fec/C00719294/schedule_b-2026-10-18T10_21_28.csv:8328`

There's no hotel in Pflugerville, no venue rental, no printed event materials. One meal is the only campaign-trail line item in the data.

The surrounding days' charges sketch out the trip. All are itemized sub-items behind the same corporate Amex, all from the same late-January window:

| Date | Vendor | City | Amount | Citation (`C00719294/schedule_b`) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-01-30 | Four Seasons Hotel | Austin, TX | $20.00 + $316.30 | `:6464`, `:7792` |
| 2026-01-31 | Shell (gas) | Brady, TX | $4.31 | `:6150` |
| 2026-01-31 | **Springhill Restaurant** | **Pflugerville, TX** | **$756.42** | `:8328` |
| 2026-02-01 | Pinthouse Pizza | Round Rock, TX | $21.32 + $34.30 | `:6489`, `:6753` |
| 2026-02-02 | JW Marriott | Austin, TX | $229.18 | `:7552` |

Brady sits almost exactly on US-87 between San Angelo, where the campaign is headquartered, and Austin. A $4.31 gas-station charge there isn't a fill-up. It's the kind of top-off charge you get from someone driving through, not flying.

Read together, the charges look like a drive from San Angelo through Central Texas: a night in Austin, the stop at Springhill in Pflugerville, a stop in Round Rock the next day, and a return through Austin. It's not a dedicated Pflugerville event so much as a brief pass through the newest part of the district. The whole swing, hotels, gas and meals included, comes to **$1,381.83**.

Two other Pflugerville entries appear in the campaign's filings, and neither involves Pfluger visiting. The campaign reports contributions to **"Pat McCord for Pflugerville Mayor"**: **$500.00** on 2025-11-01 and **$1,000.00** on 2025-12-11 (`C00719294/schedule_b:8100`, `:8477`). Both are filed as memo entries, which normally means they don't count toward the report's own totals. The filing doesn't say why.

On the receipts side, the campaign's donor records hold a single Pflugerville-address contributor across every cycle: **$300** from one donor in 2021–2022, years before the town joined the district.

## What kind of place Springhill is

Springhill Restaurant has operated in Pflugerville since 1985. It's a Southern comfort-food buffet whose whole identity is built around fried catfish; its own website is `springhillcatfish.net`. The menu runs about $14.99 for a catfish dinner salad, and there's a family pack of 20 fried fillets for groups. A $756.42 tab there is a big group order, the kind of check that comes from a few dozen people working through a catfish buffet. It isn't a private dining room or a five-course tasting menu.

Compare that with where the same committees' money goes in the companion report:
- **The Capital Grille:** $90,570.39 across 125 visits.
- **Del Frisco's Double Eagle Steakhouse:** a single $27,582.00 charge.
- **Stein Eriksen Lodge** at Deer Valley: $43,959.03 in one "facility rental and catering" line.
- **Baltusrol Golf Club,** the private New Jersey course with a roughly $150,000 initiation fee: $16,988.10.
- **The InterContinental Willard:** $48,387.24 over the Fourth of July week of 2026.

Any one of those single line items dwarfs the entire Central Texas swing that included the Pflugerville stop. The one Stein Eriksen Lodge charge alone is **32 times** the whole trip's cost.

## Where the campaign's Austin-area money goes

Pflugerville itself is one meal. The wider set of new communities (Austin, Pflugerville, Round Rock, Cedar Park, Leander) shows more activity. But most of the money goes to Austin-based professionals, not voters:
- the strategy firm **Norfleet Strategies**: $92,566.96 in the 2026 cycle;
- the law firm **Lex Politica**: $33,817.50;
- a fundraising consultant;
- hotel rooms.

The outreach that does reach the new territory shows up late. In the third quarter of 2026 the campaign gave:
- $3,000 to the **Williamson County Republican Party**;
- $2,000 to the **Travis County Republican Party**;
- $1,000 each to **Leander Area Republican Women** and **NW Austin Republican Women**.

## The math, plainly

- **Pflugerville campaign-trail spending in the data:** one meal, $756.42
- **The whole Central Texas swing that included it:** $1,381.83
- **Contributions to a Pflugerville mayoral candidate:** $1,500, filed as memo entries
- **One Stein Eriksen Lodge charge:** $43,959.03, which is 58 times the Pflugerville meal and 32 times the whole swing
- **One Fourth of July week at the Willard:** $48,387.24
- **Total luxury spending across the same committees, 2020–2026:** $1,509,708.83 (see [2026-luxury-spending.md](2026-luxury-spending.md))

## What this says

This is one data point. It would be overclaiming to say a single restaurant receipt proves a member of Congress "doesn't care" about a town. A filing record shows only what got itemized on a campaign card, not every phone call, staff visit or robocall a district received.

But as far as the paper trail goes, Pfluger's committees show:
- one identifiable stop in Pflugerville, worth about three-quarters of one steakhouse dinner;
- two small checks to a local mayoral race;
- a handful of late-cycle donations to Republican clubs in the surrounding county.

On the other side is a documented pattern of five- and six-figure spending at ski lodges, private golf clubs, historic hotels and downtown Washington steakhouses for the donor class that funds the campaign. The voters redistricting added to TX-11 didn't choose this representative. On this evidence, they haven't yet seen the campaign's money follow them.

**Limitations:**

- This report rests on a single data point from one keyword search. It can't rule out outreach that never appears in a campaign committee's disbursement filings, such as calls, mail, or district staff visits. It shouldn't be read as a complete account of Pfluger's engagement with Pflugerville.
- The Brady/Austin/Round Rock route is a circumstantial reconstruction from nearby charges, not a confirmed itinerary. No single record states "this is August Pfluger's driving route." It's a plausible reading of the data, labeled as such above.
- All of these charges are on a shared campaign card. The data can't distinguish which staff member(s) traveled versus the candidate himself.
- The two Pat McCord contributions are filed as memo entries with no explanation. Their accounting treatment (why they're memo-coded, and from which account they were paid) isn't determinable from the data.
